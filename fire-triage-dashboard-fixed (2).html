Recency decays over 72 hours; brightness / FRP use dataset-relative scaling; repeat detections count nearby observations within and 10 km. Missing values do not imply low real-world risk.

LIMITATIONS · ANALYTICAL TRIAGE ONLY

Satellite thermal detections are not ground-truth confirmation and may be affected by cloud cover, sensor conditions, or other heat sources. Missing detections do not prove the absence of fire. This prototype is not an emergency-response system; scores are not verified severity ratings.
